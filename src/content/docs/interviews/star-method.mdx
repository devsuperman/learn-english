## Explanation

Behavioral questions start with phrases like *"Tell me about a time when…"* or *"Give me an example of…"*. Answer them with a short story in four parts:

| Part | What to say | Tense |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **S**ituation | The context, in 1–2 sentences. | Past simple / past continuous |
| **T**ask | Your responsibility or the goal. | Past simple |
| **A**ction | What **you** did. This is the longest part. | Past simple |
| **R**esult | The outcome, with numbers if possible, plus what you learned. | Past simple / present perfect |

### Example: "Tell me about a time you solved a difficult problem."

> **S:** At my last job, our checkout page was timing out during Black Friday.
> **T:** I was responsible for finding the cause and fixing it before the next sale.
> **A:** I added tracing, found a slow database query, added an index, and introduced caching for product prices.
> **R:** Response time dropped from 8 seconds to 300 milliseconds, and we had zero timeouts in the next sale. I learned to add observability *before* problems happen.

## Key phrases

- *At my previous company, we were dealing with …*
- *My role was to …*
- *The first thing I did was … Then I …*
- *As a result, …* / *In the end, …*
- *What I learned from this was …*

## Common mistakes

| ❌ Don't say | ✅ Say | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **We** fixed it. *(for the whole story)* | **I** investigated … and **my team** deployed … | Interviewers want to know what **you** did. |
| It **was** very good. | Latency **dropped by 90%**. | Use numbers when you can. |
| Then I **realize** the problem. | Then I **realized** the problem. | Tell the story in the past tense. |

## Common behavioral questions

- Tell me about a time you disagreed with a teammate.
- Tell me about a mistake you made and how you handled it.
- Describe a project you are proud of.
- Tell me about a time you had to learn something quickly.
- Tell me about a time you had a tight deadline.
