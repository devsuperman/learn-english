## Explanation

The recruiter screen usually ends with practical questions: **salary, start date, location, and work authorization**. These conversations are short, but they are very important, and many people lose money because they don't have the words ready.

### Salary

- **Research first.** Know the market range for your role, level, and location.
- **Give a range**, with your target near the bottom of it.
- **Ask for their range** when you can.
- Say whether your number is **gross** (before taxes) or **net** (after taxes), and **monthly** or **annual**. Most international companies talk in **annual gross** numbers.
- For contractors, say if it's an **hourly rate** or a **monthly rate**.

### Useful vocabulary

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **base salary** | The fixed salary, without bonuses |
| **total compensation** (TC) | Base + bonus + equity + benefits |
| **equity / stock options** | A part of the company you receive |
| **benefits** | Health insurance, vacation, learning budget… |
| **notice period** | How long you must work after you resign |
| **contractor** vs. **employee** | Independent provider vs. on the payroll |
| **work authorization / visa sponsorship** | Legal permission to work in a country |
| **relocation package** | Help with moving to another country |
| **overlap** | The hours you work at the same time as the team |

## Key phrases

### When they ask about salary
- *Based on my research and experience, I'm looking for something in the range of **X to Y** per year.*
- *Could you share the salary range for this position?*
- *I'm flexible, depending on the whole package, including benefits and equity.*
- *Is that number gross or net?*

### Negotiating an offer
- *Thank you for the offer. I'm really excited about the role.*
- *Is there any flexibility on the base salary?*
- *Based on the market and my experience, I was expecting something closer to **X**.*
- *Could you tell me more about the benefits?*
- *Can I have a couple of days to think about it?*

### Logistics
- *My notice period is **30 days**, so I could start on **[date]**.*
- *I'm based in **[city]**, which is **UTC−3**. I can overlap with your team from **9 a.m. to 1 p.m. EST**.*
- *I'm open to relocating.* / *I'm looking for fully remote roles.*
- *I'd need visa sponsorship to work in **[country]**.* / *I'm authorized to work in…*
- *I'm currently working as a contractor.*

## Common mistakes

| ❌ Don't say | ✅ Say | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| I want **win** 5,000. | I'm looking for **5,000 a month**. | *Win* = victory. Use *earn* or *make*. |
| How much **you pay**? | **Could you share** the salary range? | Question structure + politeness. |
| I accept anything. | I'm flexible, depending on the whole package. | Don't undervalue yourself. |
| My salary **pretension** is… | My salary **expectation** is… | *Pretension* is a false friend. |
| I can start **in** Monday. | I can start **on** Monday. | *On* + days. |

## Today's task

1. Research the salary range for your role in the country or company you want.
2. Write your answers to: *What are your salary expectations?*, *What's your notice period?* and *Which time zone are you in?*
3. Say them out loud until they sound natural and confident.
