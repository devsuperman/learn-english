At the end, the interviewer usually asks: *"Do you have any questions for us?"* **Always say yes.** Good questions show interest and help you decide whether you want the job.

## Key phrases

**About the role**
- *What does a typical day look like for this role?*
- *What would you expect me to achieve in the first 90 days?*

**About the team and process**
- *How is the team structured, and who would I work with most closely?*
- *How do you handle code reviews and deployments?*
- *How do you make technical decisions?*

**About growth and culture**
- *How do you support learning and career growth?*
- *What do you enjoy most about working here?*

**About next steps**
- *What are the next steps in the process?*

## Common mistakes

| ❌ Don't say | ✅ Say |
| --- | --- |
| No, everything is clear. | Yes, I'd love to know … |
| **How much is** the salary? *(in a first technical round)* | Ask the recruiter: *Could you share the salary range for this role?* |
| **Which is** the stack? | **What's** the tech stack? |
