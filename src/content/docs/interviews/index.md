Most tech interviews follow the same stages. This module gives you a **ready answer** for each of them.

| Stage | What they want to know | Lesson |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Recruiter screen | Who you are, why this job, salary, visa | [Tell me about yourself](./tell-me-about-yourself/) |
| Behavioral interview | How you work with people and problems | [The STAR method](./star-method/) |
| Technical interview | How you think and communicate | Thinking out loud *(coming soon)* |
| Final stage | Culture, motivation, questions | [Questions to ask the interviewer](./questions-to-ask/) |

:::tip
Write your answers, record them, and listen back. Then practice them **without reading**. Prepare key points, not a script you memorize word for word.
:::
