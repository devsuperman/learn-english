## Explanation

| Article | Use | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **a / an** | One thing, **not specific**, mentioned for the first time. Also for **jobs**. | *I found **a** bug. I'm **a** developer.* |
| **the** | A **specific** thing that both people know | ***The** bug is in the login page.* |
| **no article** | General ideas, plural or uncountable nouns in general | ***Testing** is important. I like **APIs**.* |

**a** or **an**? It depends on the **sound**, not the letter: ***a** user* (sounds like "you"), ***an** API* (sounds like "ay"), ***an** hour*, ***a** UI*, ***an** SQL query* ("ess-queue-el").

### No article with…

- Languages and technologies, when you talk about them in general: *I use **Python** and **Docker**.*
- Names of companies: *I work at **Google**.*
- General statements: ***Code reviews** help **teams** learn.*

## Key phrases

- *I'm **a** software engineer at **a** fintech startup.*
- *We have **an** issue with **the** payment service.*
- ***The** team decided to use **an** event-driven architecture.*
- *I have **a** question about **the** new endpoint.*

## Common mistakes

| ❌ Don't say | ✅ Say | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| I'm developer. | I'm **a** developer. | Jobs take *a/an*. |
| **The** Python is my favorite language. | Python is my favorite language. | No article with languages in general. |
| I work at **the** Microsoft. | I work at Microsoft. | No article with company names. |
| I found **a** error. | I found **an** error. | *Error* starts with a vowel sound. |
| **The** documentation is important. *(in general)* | Documentation is important. | General ideas don't take *the*. |
