You don't need to know *all* English grammar to work in tech. You need about **ten structures**, and you need to use them correctly and automatically. This module teaches them through sentences you will actually say at work.

## Quick reference

| Situation | Structure | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Routine, facts | Present simple | *I **work** on the payments team.* |
| Right now, temporary | Present continuous | *I'**m working** on the login bug.* |
| Finished time (yesterday, in 2022) | Past simple | *I **joined** the company in 2022.* |
| Experience, until now | Present perfect | *I'**ve used** Kubernetes for three years.* |
| Plan already decided | Going to | *I'**m going to** refactor this module.* |
| Decision now, promise | Will | *I'**ll** check it after lunch.* |
| Yes/no question | Do/does/did + subject + verb | ***Do** you **use** TypeScript?* |
| Polite request | Could you + verb | ***Could you** review my PR?* |

:::tip[How to study grammar]
Don't memorize rules. Learn **one example sentence** for each structure by heart, about your own work. When you're not sure, compare what you want to say with your example.
:::
