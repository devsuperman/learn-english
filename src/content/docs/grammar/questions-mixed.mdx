## Explanation

Ask yourself these questions, in this order:

1. **Is the main verb *be* (*am, is, are, was, were*), a modal (*can, could, will, should*), or *have* + past participle?**
   Then you **don't use** *do/does/did*: *"**Is** it ready?"*, *"**Can** you help?"*, *"**Have** you tested it?"*
2. **Is it about the past?** → **did** (for every subject)
3. **Is it about the present, with *he, she, it* or a singular noun?** → **does**
4. **Is it about the present, with *I, you, we, they* or a plural noun?** → **do**

| | I, you, we, they, plural nouns | he, she, it, singular nouns |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Present** (habits, facts) | **do** | **does** |
| **Past** (finished actions) | **did** | **did** |

After all three helpers, the main verb is in the **base form**: *do you **work**, does she **work**, did they **work***.

**Clue words:**
- *every day, usually, often, always, in general* → **do / does**
- *yesterday, last week, this morning, ago, in 2022* → **did**

**Here's the same question in three ways:**

- ***Do** you **deploy** on Fridays?* means "Is it your habit?" (subject: *you*).
- ***Does** the team **deploy** on Fridays?* means "Is it the team's habit?" (*the team* = *it*).
- ***Did** you **deploy** on Friday?* means "Last Friday, one time in the past."

## Key phrases

- *Do you have any questions for us?*
- *How does the team handle on-call?*
- *Did you have a chance to look at my PR?*
- *What do you like most about this job?*
- *Does that make sense?*
- *Why did the build fail?*

## Common mistakes

| ❌ Don't say | ✅ Say | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Does** you have a minute? | **Do** you have a minute? | *You* → *do*. |
| **Do** the app support SSO? | **Does** the app support SSO? | *The app* = *it* → *does*. |
| **Do** you finish it yesterday? | **Did** you finish it yesterday? | *Yesterday* → *did*. |
| Does she **works** / Did you **worked** | Does she **work** / Did you **work** | Base verb after all three. |
| **Did** the release **was** on time? | **Was** the release on time? | With *be*, no helper. |
| How long **does** the tests take? | How long **do** the tests take? | *The tests* is plural → *do*. |

## Today's task

**Record yourself** (on your phone is fine) asking **six questions about your real work**:

- two with ***do***: *"Do we have a design doc for this?"*
- two with ***does***: *"How does the retry logic work?"*
- two with ***did***: *"Did the hotfix go out last night?"*

Listen to your recording and check two things for each question: the helper is **before the subject**, and the main verb is in the **base form**.
