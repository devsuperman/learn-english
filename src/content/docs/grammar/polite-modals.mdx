## Explanation

In international teams, **tone** matters as much as grammar. Direct sentences like *"Review my PR"* can sound rude in English, even if they are normal in your language. **Modal verbs** make your sentences softer.

| Modal | Use | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **could** | Polite requests, possibilities | ***Could** you review my PR?* |
| **would** | Very polite requests, preferences | ***Would** you mind joining the call?* / *I**'d** prefer to…* |
| **should** | Advice, recommendations | *We **should** add a test for this.* |
| **might / may** | Possibility, soft suggestions | *This **might** cause a memory leak.* |
| **can** | Ability, informal requests | ***Can** you hear me?* |

After a modal, use the **base verb without "to"**: *could **help*** (not *could to help*).

### Softening disagreement

- *I see your point, but **wouldn't** it be simpler to…?*
- ***Could** we consider using a queue here?*
- *I **might** be wrong, but I think…*
- *It **might** be worth adding a cache.*

## Key phrases

- *Could you share your screen?*
- *Would you mind taking a look at this?* (+ **-ing**)
- *Would it be possible to move the meeting?*
- *I'd like to suggest a different approach.*
- *Maybe we should split this PR into two.*

## Common mistakes

| ❌ Don't say | ✅ Say | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Review my PR. | **Could you** review my PR, please? | Commands sound rude. |
| Could you **to** help me? | Could you **help** me? | No *to* after modals. |
| Would you mind **to check** it? | Would you mind **checking** it? | *Would you mind* + **-ing**. |
| You **must** change this. *(code review)* | You **might want to** change this. / **Could** we change this? | *Must* sounds too strong. |
| I **want** a coffee break. | I**'d like** a coffee break. | *I'd like* is more polite. |
