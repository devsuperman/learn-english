## Explanation

Many people **read** tech words every day but rarely **say** them. Then, in an interview, they pronounce *cache* or *queue* in a way the interviewer doesn't understand.

Click the 🔊 buttons to hear each word. The sound comes from your browser's built-in voice, so it may vary a little between devices. The **"sounds like"** column uses simple English spelling, and **capital letters** show the stressed syllable.

## Word stress

In English, **one syllable is stronger** than the others. Wrong stress makes words hard to recognize, even when the sounds are correct.

| Word | Stress | Listen |
| --- | --- | --- |
| develop / developer | de-**VEL**-op / de-**VEL**-op-er | <Say text="develop, developer" label="" /> |
| component | com-**PO**-nent | <Say text="component" label="" /> |
| architecture | **AR**-chi-tec-ture | <Say text="architecture" label="" /> |
| algorithm | **AL**-go-rithm | <Say text="algorithm" label="" /> |
| analysis / analyze | a-**NAL**-y-sis / **AN**-a-lyze | <Say text="analysis, analyze" label="" /> |
| deploy | de-**PLOY** | <Say text="deploy" label="" /> |
| technology | tech-**NOL**-o-gy | <Say text="technology" label="" /> |
| a **RE**cord / to re**CORD** | noun: first syllable / verb: second syllable | <Say text="a record, to record" label="" /> |

## The -ed ending

The past tense **-ed** has three sounds. Most learners say "-ed" as an extra syllable every time. That's usually **wrong**.

| Sound | When | Examples | Listen |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| /t/ | after p, k, s, sh, ch, f | fixed, pushed, checked → "fixt", "pusht" | <Say text="fixed, pushed, checked" label="" /> |
| /d/ | after voiced sounds and vowels | deployed, merged, called → "deployd" | <Say text="deployed, merged, called" label="" /> |
| /ɪd/ (extra syllable) | **only** after t or d | tested, updated, added → "TEST-id" | <Say text="tested, updated, added" label="" /> |
