## Explanation

A standup update answers three questions in **under one minute**:

1. **What did I do yesterday?** → past simple
2. **What will I do today?** → *going to* / *will* / present continuous
3. **Is anything blocking me?** → *blocked by*, *waiting for*, *need help with*

Keep it short. Details go in a separate conversation after the standup.

## Key phrases

- *Yesterday I **wrapped up** the payment integration.*
- *Today I'm **picking up** the ticket about notifications.*
- *I'm **waiting on** a review from the platform team.*
- *I **could use some help** with the Docker setup. Can someone pair with me after the standup?*
- *No blockers on my side.*
- *Let's **take this offline**.* (= let's discuss it later, not in this meeting)

## Common mistakes

| ❌ Don't say | ✅ Say |
| --- | --- |
| I **stayed** working on the bug. | I **kept** working on the bug. / I **was still** working on the bug. |
| I **have** a doubt about the ticket. | I **have a question** about the ticket. |
| I will **realize** the deploy. | I will **do** the deploy. / I will **deploy** it. |

:::tip[Grammar for this lesson]
See [Future: will, going to, present continuous](../../grammar/future/) for today's plans, and [Polite modals](../../grammar/polite-modals/) for asking for help.
:::
