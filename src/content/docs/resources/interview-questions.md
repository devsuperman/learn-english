Use this list for mock interviews. Ask a friend to read the questions to you, or record yourself answering them.

## Recruiter screen
- Tell me about yourself.
- Why are you looking for a new job?
- Why do you want to work here?
- What are your salary expectations?
- When can you start?
- Do you need visa sponsorship?

## Experience
- Walk me through a project you're proud of.
- What was your role in that project?
- What technologies did you use, and why?
- What was the hardest technical problem you've solved?

## Behavioral (use [STAR](../../interviews/star-method/))
- Tell me about a time you disagreed with a teammate.
- Tell me about a mistake you made.
- Tell me about a time you had a tight deadline.
- How do you handle feedback?
- Tell me about a time you helped someone on your team.

## Technical communication
- Can you explain how this code works?
- How would you design a URL shortener?
- What trade-offs did you consider?
- How would you test this?
- What would you do differently with more time?

- Do you have any questions for us? → see [Questions to ask](../../interviews/questions-to-ask/)
